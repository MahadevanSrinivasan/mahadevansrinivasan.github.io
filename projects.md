- #### [Apex Nitro 3D: Street & Canyon Racing](/apex-nitro/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/apex-nitro))
A wholesome, high-speed 3D WebGL street & canyon racer built with **Three.js PBR + UnrealBloom** post-processing! Opens onto a live 6-car **Tokyo Neon Attract Race** before entering the 360° **Underground Speedshop** (**6 sculpted supercars**, clear-coat reflection paints, 5-spoke rims, radial neon underglows, Aero Wing kits). Race across **3 continuous spline circuits** (**Tokyo Neon Underground**, **Pacific Sunset Canyon**, **Alpine Horizon Speedway**) in **Underground Circuit**, **Drift Touge**, or **Hot Pursuit** modes with weight-transfer drift physics, 6-speed **Auto/Manual** gearbox, **Perfect Launch** RPM zone, aerodynamic slipstream drafting, **Cockpit** steering-wheel view, live **3D Rear-View Mirror**, wet asphalt reflections, and desktop + mobile touch controls.
- #### [Super Leo City: Hero Patrol & Stunt Sandbox](/super-leo-city/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/super-leo-city))
A wholesome, non-violent **3D WebGL** open-world driving, rescue, and stunt sandbox for kids! Explore 6 districts—including **Custom ZIP-Code Towns & 3D Elevation Terrains** powered by OpenStreetMap + Open-Elevation—on foot as Junior Mayor Leo or hop in and out of **8 vehicles + 1 rideable Dino** (**Comet GT Sports Car**, **Big Red Fire Engine** with working Water Cannon, **Hero Patrol Cruiser**, **Melody Ice Cream Van**, **Rocket Pizza Buggy**, **Monster Bounce Truck**, **Aqua-Jet Speedboat**, **Sky-Rescue Helicopter**, and **Emerald Rex**) across rotating Hero Missions, mega stunt ramps, and a Central Park car-soccer pitch.
- #### [Excitebike (Classic 1984 NES 8-Bit Edition)](/super-dirt-bike-hd/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/super-dirt-bike-hd))
An authentic 8-bit pixel-art recreation of the 1984 NES classic *Excitebike*—featuring the original green/tan 4-lane stadium track, discrete trapezoidal ramps with diagonal red/white & blue/yellow striped skirts (Obstacles A–I), mud pits, cooling arrows (`>>`), the bottom `TEMP` bar with `OVER HEAT` stalls, rider recovery from the grass shoulder, and all three original modes (**Selection A**, **Selection B** vs. CPU rivals, and **Design** mode)—playable on the web and natively on RetroPie.
- #### [Super Leo World HD: Dino Island](/super-leo-world-hd/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/super-leo-world-hd))
A 1080p (1920×1080) 60 FPS high-definition platformer featuring 4 rideable Dino companions inspired by *Super Mario World* — **Emerald Rex** (Chameleon Tongue-Grab & Flutter-Jump hover), **Blaze Rex** (spits 3x giant fireballs), **Sky Rex** (winged multi-flap flight), and **Quake Rex** (seismic shockwave stomp) — plus wall-jumps, ground-pounds, bail-out save jumps, and 8 HD stages.
- #### [Super Leo Bros.](/super-leo-bros/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/super-leo-bros))
An original classic 2D platformer written in a single C99 file with zero external asset files—compiled natively for Raspberry Pi (RetroPie / SDL 1.2) and to WebAssembly for the browser. Features momentum & skidding physics, 3-tier power-ups (Mushroom, Fire Flower, Starman), kickable shells, an enterable secret coin pipe, checkpoints, and 12 stages across 3 worlds.
- #### [Spelling Bee](/spellingbee/)
A minimalist, distraction-free Spelling Bee preparation web app for 3rd–5th graders. Features natural browser pronunciation via the Web Speech API, four difficulty tiers, streak bonuses, 10-word practice rounds with targeted review, and Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) letter-tile diffing to pinpoint exact spelling edits.
- #### [MathClub](/mathclub/)
A fun, interactive arithmetic typing adventure for kids! Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division through timed challenges and survival modes. Features a level progression system, combo multipliers, performance tracking with charts, and a fully customizable practice playground.
- #### [Color Sudoku for Kids](/colorsudoku/)
A playful, vibrant twist on classic Sudoku designed for younger learners. Solve puzzles using bright colors and symbols instead of numbers to build spatial and logical reasoning skills.
- #### [Pokémon Card Cycler](/pokemoncycler/)
A mobile-friendly card viewing and cycling companion app for Pokémon trading cards, featuring set browsing, smooth transitions, and instant card inspection.
- #### [CSV Budget Analyzer](/budget_analyzer/)
A privacy-first, client-side budgeting tool. Upload bank or credit card CSV exports to automatically categorize transactions, visualize spending breakdowns with interactive charts, and analyze expense trends without sending your financial data to any server.
- #### [Recurring Expenses Tracker](/recurring_expenses_tracker/)
A lightweight dashboard to track recurring subscriptions, class packs, and routine expense payments, complete with overdue indicators, balance counters, and history logs.
- #### [8-Queens Puzzle](/nqueens.html) ([WebAssembly Version](/nqueens_wasm.html))
An animated visual solver for the classic 8-Queens chess puzzle, illustrating backtracking search solutions in real time in pure JavaScript and WebAssembly.

- #### [Podcast Downloader](https://github.com/MahadevanSrinivasan/podcastdownloader)
This is a side project which is designed to automatically download your podcasts as and when they become available. Fully written in python, this uses BeautifulSoup to parse the podcast websites and downloads the MP3s to your laptop/desktop. Listen on!
