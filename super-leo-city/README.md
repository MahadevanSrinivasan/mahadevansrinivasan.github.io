# Super Leo City: Hero Patrol & Stunt Sandbox

A wholesome, kid-friendly open-world driving, rescue, and stunt sandbox (inspired by *LEGO City Undercover* and classic top-down *Simpsons: Hit & Run* / *GTA* driving physics) with **zero violence**!

- **Play Online (HTML5 Canvas 60FPS)**: [https://theshybulb.com/super-leo-city/](https://theshybulb.com/super-leo-city/)
- **Native RetroPie / Linux Port**: `super_leo_city.c` (C99 + SDL2 Streaming Texture Renderer at locked 60 FPS)

## Highlights
- **Hop In & Out of 8 Vehicles + 1 Rideable Dino (`E` / `Enter`)**:
  1. **Comet GT Sports Car**: High-speed drifting + Nitro Boost (`Space`).
  2. **Big Red Fire Engine**: Flashing lights, siren & **High-Pressure Water Cannon (`Z`)** to put out city fires and pop hydrant fountains!
  3. **Hero Patrol Cruiser**: Flashing lights & rescue beam to catch runaway toy Robo-Pups.
  4. **Melody Ice Cream Van**: Plays a musical jingle while driving and launches rainbow ice cream cones (`Z`) to cheering kids!
  5. **Rocket Pizza Buggy**: Launches hot pizzas (`Z`) into hungry picnic zones.
  6. **Monster Bounce Truck**: Giant tires that bounce over obstacles and get 2x height off yellow stunt ramps!
  7. **Aqua-Jet Speedboat**: Docked at the Sunny Beach Marina—drive right across the bay and river canals!
  8. **Sky-Rescue Helicopter**: Parked at the Airport Helipad—fly right over skyscrapers, trees, and water!
  9. **Emerald Rex (Rideable Dino)**: Waiting in Dino Safari Park!
- **Central Park Soccer Mini-Game**: Drive any car into the giant soccer ball on the Central Park pitch to score a **GOOOOOAL (+1000)** with confetti bursts!
- **5 Rotating Hero Missions + Mini-Map Radar & Golden Compass Arrow**.
