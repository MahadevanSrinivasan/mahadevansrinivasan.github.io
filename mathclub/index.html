Left-to-Right Split (82 × 3)

82 × 3 → (80 × 3) + (2 × 3) = 240 + 6 = 246

In your head, multiply from LEFT to RIGHT! Do the tens first (80 × 3 = 240), then add the ones (2 × 3 = 6).
